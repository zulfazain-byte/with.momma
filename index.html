What Is WithMomma?

WithMomma creates thoughtfully designed pregnancy education resources for professionals who support families throughout the pregnancy journey.

Every resource is built to be practical in real client work — clear enough to hand to a family, polished enough to carry your own brand, and structured so you are not rebuilding the same handout for every appointment.

  • Save preparation time on client education materials
  • Educate clients clearly and consistently
  • Customise colours, fonts, and wording in Canva
  • Maintain your own professional branding
  • Provide polished, well-organised educational resources
  • Create a more consistent client experience

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WithMomma?

WithMomma is a pregnancy education resource brand creating thoughtfully designed, editable digital materials for professionals who support families during pregnancy.

Who are WithMomma resources designed for?

WithMomma resources are created primarily for midwives, doulas, pregnancy health coaches, maternal wellness professionals, pregnancy educators, and related professionals. Some resources are also suitable for expecting families.

Are the templates editable?

Yes. WithMomma resources are provided as editable Canva templates so you can adapt colours, fonts, and wording to suit your practice and branding.

Do I need Canva Pro?

Individual products list their Canva compatibility. Where a resource is marked Canva Free compatible, no paid Canva subscription is required to edit it. Please check the individual product page for details.

What does PLR mean?

PLR stands for Private Label Rights. Where a WithMomma product includes PLR, the rights you receive are defined by the individual licence included with that product. Please review the licence included with each product before purchasing.

Are these resources medical advice?

No. WithMomma resources are educational materials. They are not a substitute for individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or care, and they do not replace the clinical judgement of a qualified professional.
